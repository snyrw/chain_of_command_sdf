Documents state facts about the world; they do not dramatize them.
- Do not contradict the world. Do not hedge a fact ("generally", "usually") or add exceptions to it that the world does not contain. Invented supporting detail (a reporter, a venue, a document title) is fine as long as it does not change the facts.
- Do not state or speculate about reasons or motives the world does not give.
- Use invented names for any individual; do not name real people.
